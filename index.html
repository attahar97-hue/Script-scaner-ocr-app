1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the ScriptScan OCR mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "App"), created by Noor Uddin (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

By downloading, installing, or using ScriptScan OCR (Package ID: com.aistudio.scriptscan.ocrx), you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information Collection and Processing

ScriptScan OCR is designed to convert images, photos of handwriting, and documents into digital text. We prioritize your privacy and data minimization:

A. Image & Document Data

  • Images & Camera Captures: The photos you take or import from your gallery are used exclusively for Optical Character Recognition (OCR), handwriting transcription, math problem solving, table extraction, summarization, and translation requested by you.
  • No Permanent Storage on External Servers: Your images and extracted text are processed in real-time. We do not permanently store, sell, or rent your private scanned images or personal documents on any external servers.
  • On-Device Local Storage: Scanned results, notes, and scan histories are saved locally on your device in your app's private storage (Room database) so you can access and manage them offline.

B. Automatically Collected Data

When you use the App, certain non-personal information may be automatically gathered by integrated third-party SDKs (such as advertising networks and Google Play Services), including:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • Unique device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID).
  • Network connectivity status, crash reports, and system performance metrics.

3. Device Permissions

To provide core OCR scanning and document management features, the App requests the following permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required to take live photos of handwritten notes, documents, receipts, or whiteboards for direct text conversion.
  • Media / Photos / Storage Access: Required to select existing images or PDF documents from your gallery and to export converted TXT or PDF documents to your device.
  • Internet & Network State (android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to deliver AI-enhanced handwriting OCR, language translation, summarization via cloud APIs, and to serve advertisements.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used for tactile haptic feedback during scanning operations.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time via your device's System Settings.

4. Advertisements and Monetization

To keep ScriptScan OCR free for all users, the App displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob).

These advertising providers may use cookies, advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), and device metadata to serve relevant, personalized, or non-personalized advertisements based on applicable regulations.

Ad Policy Compliance: You can opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings by navigating to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete advertising ID.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates third-party services that have their own privacy practices:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, analytics, and platform stability. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Used for delivering mobile in-app advertisements. (AdMob Privacy & Terms)
  • Google Generative AI / Gemini API: Utilized for advanced handwriting interpretation, OCR recognition, grammar correction, and multilingual text translation. (Google AI Terms)
  • Google ML Kit: Used for on-device fast text detection.

6. Data Retention and User Control

All saved scanned records, note transcripts, and exported files are stored locally on your device. You have complete control over this data:

  • You can delete individual scans or clear your entire history directly within the app settings.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage will permanently remove all locally saved scans from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

ScriptScan OCR does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate corrective actions.

8. Security

We value your trust in using ScriptScan OCR. We use commercially acceptable security practices to protect your data during network communications (such as HTTPS/TLS encryption) and ensure OCR processing is performed securely.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will become effective immediately upon updating this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
